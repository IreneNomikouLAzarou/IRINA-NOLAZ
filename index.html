About Us

Since 2012, IRINA NOLAZ Studio has been creating intentional jewelry that speaks to the soul. Each piece begins as an idea drawn from nature, a color palette, a shape, an emotion waiting to be captured in precious and semi-precious stones, silver, enamel, resin, and hand-inlaid mosaic. We believe that jewelry is not decoration it is a conversation between maker and wearer, a small object chosen to hold meaning. Our process is entirely handmade and hand-drawn. Every design, every line, every stone placement emerges from the artist's hand with intention and care. We create in small batches because mass production cannot hold the depth we seek. Each piece carries its own story—not one we impose, but one our clients choose to live with. We work with colors that generate feelings, the warmth of amber against skin, the calm of turquoise, the strength of deep indigo. We craft pieces for everyday wear, moments of quiet beauty, objects that grow more meaningful the longer you know them. Our jewelry asks nothing of its wearer except honesty, the willingness to choose something that resonates, that feels true. This is wearable art born from a simple belief, that beauty and intention belong in the moments we live through every day.
